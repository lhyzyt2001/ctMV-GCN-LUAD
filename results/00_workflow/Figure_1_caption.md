**Figure 1. Overview of the robustness-aware scTDA-GCN workflow for lung adenocarcinoma gene prioritization.** (A) Aggregated cell-type expression profiles from TISCH were used as node features and to construct a mutual cosine 12-nearest-neighbour cell-profile similarity graph. High-confidence STRING v12 functional associations (combined score ≥700) defined the STRING view, while size-weighted shared KEGG Medicus pathways followed by top-12 sparsification defined the pathway view. Open Targets LUAD associations with `maxClinicalTrialPhase > 0` supplied the clinical-evidence endpoint, yielding 1,184 positive genes among 25,242 graph nodes. (B) Within cross-validation, feature standardization and five-component principal component analysis were fitted using training nodes. Three two-layer weighted graph convolutional branches generated 20-dimensional STRING, cell-profile and pathway embeddings. Node-level local attention assigned gene-specific weights to the three views; the weighted embeddings were concatenated into a 60-dimensional representation and classified using a linear softmax layer with class-weighted cross-entropy. (C) Performance was assessed by repeated five-fold transductive cross-validation with three repeats and an inner validation split. Test labels were hidden, whereas all nodes and disease-independent graph topology remained visible. A ten-seed final ensemble generated mean scores, rank-stability estimates, local-attention weights and channel-ablation effects. Post hoc degree-aware analyses included degree-stratified evaluation, 1:1 degree-matched bootstrap comparisons, conditional permutation, nonlinear degree residualization and edge-dropout sensitivity. The robustness-aware top 20 was selected without external outcomes and subsequently characterized using TCGA-LUAD, GSEA, GSE68465, CPTAC tumor protein profiles and DepMap LUAD CRISPR dependency data. Downstream associations were interpreted as computational characterization and not as evidence of therapeutic efficacy or causality. Abbreviations: AUPRC, area under the precision–recall curve; BH, Benjamini–Hochberg; CV, cross-validation; GAT, graph attention network; GCN, graph convolutional network; GSEA, gene set enrichment analysis; LUAD, lung adenocarcinoma; OS, overall survival; PCA, principal component analysis; PyG, PyTorch Geometric; RF, random forest; ROC-AUC, area under the receiver operating characteristic curve; RWR, random walk with restart.
